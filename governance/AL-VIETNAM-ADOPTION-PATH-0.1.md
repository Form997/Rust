# AL Vietnam Adoption and International Alignment Path 0.1

- Document ID: AL-VN-ADOPTION-001
- Version: 0.1.0
- Status: Planning framework; no formal adoption or certification claimed
- Date: 2026-11-27

## 1. Objective

Build AL in a way that can be evaluated against relevant international practice and law while remaining usable and responsible in Vietnam. The project must distinguish voluntary engineering alignment, legal compliance, national standard adoption, certification and formal government procurement/acceptance. These are not interchangeable.

## 2. Workstreams

1. **Legal inventory:** identify applicable Vietnamese laws, decrees, circulars and competent authorities based on AL's actual activities, data, users and deployments.
2. **National standards inventory:** identify relevant TCVN and QCVN, their current editions, amendments, mandatory/voluntary status and official adoption records.
3. **International mapping:** map relevant ISO/IEC, IEEE, NIST, W3C, IETF and intergovernmental instruments to AL controls.
4. **Language and accessibility:** validate Vietnamese, regional language variation, Unicode behavior, accessibility and multilingual quality with representative users and lawful datasets.
5. **Scientific integrity:** adopt traceable units, metrology, uncertainty and domain-specific validation where AL supports scientific work.
6. **Independent review:** seek qualified technical, security, privacy and legal review; publish scope and findings.
7. **Community and institutional engagement:** invite universities, research groups, standards experts, public agencies and industry to review proposals; do not imply endorsement without written evidence.

## 3. Adoption workflow

`IDENTIFY → VERIFY OFFICIAL TEXT → CHECK VIETNAM STATUS → ASSESS APPLICABILITY → MAP AL CONTROLS → TEST → INDEPENDENT REVIEW → APPROVE PROJECT POLICY → MONITOR`

## 4. Evidence required for claims

- “Aligned with”: documented clause/control mapping and evidence for the stated scope.
- “Conforms to”: complete applicable requirements assessment, documented exclusions and competent conformity evaluation where required.
- “Certified”: valid certificate from an authorized/recognized certification body, within scope and validity.
- “Adopted in Vietnam”: official Vietnamese adoption instrument or catalogue record.
- “Accepted by an institution”: explicit written acceptance from that institution.
- “Internationally recognized”: identifiable recognition mechanism and verifiable evidence.

Until evidence exists, use wording such as “designed with reference to”, “candidate mapping”, or “under evaluation”.

## 5. Public accountability

Publish the scope, versions, source register, limitations, open gaps, review dates and correction process. Community feedback is valuable but does not replace legal interpretation, technical verification or formal adoption.
