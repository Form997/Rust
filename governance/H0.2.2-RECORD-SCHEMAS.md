# H0.2.2 — Governance record schemas

**Status:** Proposed implementation; not an approved Rulebook policy.
**Branch:** `h0-2-2-governance-record-schemas`

## Delivered contracts

| Contract | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `metric-card.schema.json` | Defines metric identity/version, purpose, operational definition, formula and variables, unit, scale, population, measurement protocol, aggregation, uncertainty, threshold state and evidence. |
| `source-record.schema.json` | Records issuing body, instrument type, edition, jurisdiction, dates, legal/voluntary applicability, official URL verification and Vietnam adoption status. |
| `decision-record.schema.json` | Records context, selected decision, alternatives, Rulebook references, evidence, impacted components, risks, rollback and approval. |
| `training-run-manifest.schema.json` | Pins Rulebook bundle and metric-card checksums, dataset versions/provenance/license state, source commit, configuration checksum, random seeds, environment and output artifacts. |

## Validation invariants

- Every schema declares JSON Schema Draft 2020-12.
- A threshold may remain `NOT_SET`; an approved threshold must carry a non-null value and at least one evidence identifier.
- An approved decision must have an approved approval record, named approver and timestamp.
- Training and fine-tuning manifests require an approved Rulebook, verified dataset license state and approved governance review. Evaluation-only fixtures may retain unknown dataset rights and must not be misrepresented as training.
- The training manifest records checksums and provenance references; it does not itself verify that those hashes match downloaded bytes or that dataset rights are valid.

## Fixtures and CI

Positive fixtures cover proposed metric, unverified source, proposed decision and evaluation-only run. Negative fixtures verify rejection of a bundle missing required fields and an approved metric with no approved threshold value/evidence. GitHub Actions validates all schema meta-structures and fixtures.

## Known limits

JSON Schema checks structure and selected cross-field invariants only. It cannot authenticate official sources, establish scientific validity, determine legal applicability, verify dataset consent/licensing, reproduce a run, or authorize a release. Those require separate source verification, domain review, runtime checks and human approval.

The example metric's threshold is deliberately `NOT_SET`. The source example is explicitly `unverified`. The decision is `proposed`. The training manifest fixture is evaluation-only, not evidence of a real training run.
